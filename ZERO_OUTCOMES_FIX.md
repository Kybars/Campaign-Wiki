## Changes made

- `partitionGrounding` now validates each schema-valid proposition, entity, relationship, and source discrepancy separately. It retains only items with original verbatim evidence on a selected physical page and valid identities/references. Duplicate identity or proposition IDs, invalid/missing parents, cycles, unresolved subjects, wrong pages, paraphrased quotes, and invalid relationship endpoints are rejected with indexed issues. It never rewrites evidence or invents replacements. The strict `validateGrounding` entry point still reports every issue.
- The development and isolated holdout runners save every schema-valid response **before** validation: original parsed output, raw PDF.js source pages, response ID when returned, and usage. Extraction validation then records complete indexed grounding issues with output ID, page, quote, and reason; judge source-evidence validation records its failure reason. A partially valid extraction proceeds using only accepted items. A candidate with no usable narrative item is `failed_known` with specific reasons and the original response retained privately. The blinded judge sees only accepted output.
- Rejected items are counted once per rejected item as unsupported errors in the existing quality penalty; they also contribute to the general `unsupported` failure pattern. An ungrounded claim is absent from the judge candidate and cannot receive recovery credit. No development gold row, source byte, narrative weight, judge wording, initial extraction prompt, or factual quote-matching rule was changed.
- The previous `paid-progress.json` and plan were copied byte-for-byte into ignored `tournament/history/`. The historical ledger SHA-256 `83154faf8e1d81c1817c4cb318a2865bef2e3cf8b7bddeaeb5a7d32f25dced20` is pinned in the new preflight. Future development reservations and finalist/holdout handoffs include **all 10 prior dispatches and their known usage**. The new run uses a separate progress file; it cannot overwrite or silently reuse old checkpoints. The shared ceilings remain 50 paid requests and 2,000,000 actual tokens, with the USD 1.65 safeguard requiring a newly approved authorization tied to the new hash. At most 40 additional planned requests would fit the request ceiling, and the unknown tenth usage blocks dispatch entirely until reconciled.
- The preflight now requires a current passing three-source fixture result, bound to the fixture definitions, fixture harness, grounding code, scoring code, production text cleaner, and exact source payload hashes. An offline replay reports historical calls and unknown usage separately from zero **new** paid calls.
